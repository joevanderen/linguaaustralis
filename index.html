About us

Two friends, one shared passion for language.

¡Hola! We're Delfina and Luciana, the founders and teachers behind Lingua Australis.

We met while studying Linguistics and Literature in Buenos Aires, where we quickly bonded over our shared love of everything language-related.

After years of studying and working together, we decided to turn that passion into something of our own: a Spanish school where we could share our knowledge, experience, and love for the language with students from around the world.

7+years of combined teaching experience, bringing both academic expertise and a personal approach to every lesson.

Why Lingua Australis?

Our name comes from two Latin words: lingua, meaning "language," and australis, meaning "southern."

It reflects where we come from and how we approach teaching Spanish.

Spanish is spoken across many countries, each with its own accents, expressions, and traditions. Our goal is to help you understand that diversity while developing the skills and confidence to communicate naturally.

How our classes work

Lessons built around you

We know that no two students learn in exactly the same way. That's why our lessons are designed around your individual needs, interests, and learning style.

  1. 1

    Let's get to know each other

    Your journey begins with an initial assessment lesson: one 30-minute class where we'll assess your Spanish comprehension and production level.

    We'll get to know you, discuss your goals, assess your current level, and identify the areas you'd like to improve. It's also a chance for you to experience our teaching style and decide whether we're the right fit for you.

    The assessment lesson is booked and paid for separately, with no obligation to purchase a package afterward.

  2. 2

    Choose your lesson package

    Once we've established your level and goals, you can choose the package that works best for you. Every lesson lasts 50 minutes and is tailored to your individual needs.

    Depending on your level and objectives, lessons combine:

    • Grammar Understand how Spanish works and learn to use it confidently.
    • Vocabulary Build practical vocabulary for everyday situations and topics that interest you.
    • Conversation Practice speaking naturally, improve fluency, and gain confidence.
    • Listening & pronunciation Develop your comprehension and sound more natural.
    • Culture Explore the expressions, traditions, and linguistic diversity of the Spanish-speaking world.
  3. 3

    Learn at your own pace

    We believe that consistency matters, but flexibility does too.

    Our lesson packages allow you to organize your sessions according to your availability and learning goals, so you can make meaningful progress without committing to a rigid schedule.

02

Spanish Exam Preparation

Prepare with purpose. Take your exam with confidence.

Preparing for a Spanish proficiency exam? We're here to help you develop the language skills and exam strategies you need. Our private preparation lessons are tailored to your current level, target score, and exam requirements.

  • DELE Diplomas de Español como Lengua Extranjera: official Spanish proficiency diplomas awarded by Instituto Cervantes on behalf of Spain's Ministry of Education.
  • SIELE Servicio Internacional de Evaluación de la Lengua Española: an internationally recognized Spanish proficiency assessment.
  • AP Spanish Language and Culture Preparation for the College Board's Advanced Placement Spanish exam in the United States.
  • AP Spanish Literature and Culture Support with literary analysis, reading comprehension, and written responses for the AP exam.

Depending on your exam, lessons may focus on reading comprehension, writing, listening, speaking, grammar, vocabulary, and practice with exam-style exercises.

Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know any Spanish before starting?

Not at all! We work with students at different proficiency levels, from complete beginners to advanced speakers.

Are lessons taught online?

Yes! All our lessons are held online, so you can learn Spanish from wherever you are.

How long is each lesson?

Each lesson lasts 50 minutes. The initial assessment lesson lasts 30 minutes.

Can I choose how often I take lessons?

Absolutely. Our packages are designed to offer flexibility, allowing you to organize your lessons around your schedule and goals, subject to teacher availability. Each package has to be used within one month.

What happens during the first lesson?

We'll assess your Spanish level, talk about your goals, and introduce you to our teaching approach. The initial assessment is paid for separately, so you can decide whether you'd like to continue before committing to a package.

Do you teach Latin American or European Spanish?

As teachers from Argentina and Bolivia, we naturally bring a Latin American perspective to our lessons. However, we also introduce students to different Spanish varieties, helping them understand the language as it's spoken across the Spanish-speaking world.

Ready to start speaking Spanish?

Whether you're learning from scratch, preparing for an exam, or looking to take your Spanish to the next level, we'd love to be part of your journey.
